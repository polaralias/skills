# Executive slide deck template

Default to a short, decision-oriented deck. Leave deep implementation detail out unless it changes the decision.

## Slide 1: Recommendation and decision ask

- initiative name
- recommendation
- decision required
- one-line reason

## Slide 2: Business problem

- current pain
- who is affected
- business impact

## Slide 3: Proposed AI workload

- what the AI-assisted capability does
- target users
- expected output
- what is out of scope

## Slide 4: Prioritisation assessment

Show value, complexity, risk, reuse, and data readiness.

Keep it to one readable table, matrix, or summary view.

## Slide 5: POC scope

- POC objective
- included scope
- excluded scope
- success criteria

## Slide 6: MVP and production path

- MVP capabilities
- what production adds beyond MVP
- reusable building blocks

## Slide 7: Data, security, and compliance

- data required
- access position
- sensitivity
- controls and review points

## Slide 8: Risks and mitigations

Focus on the small number of risks that could materially alter the decision.

## Slide 9: Timeline and responsibilities

- discovery, POC, MVP, and production phases
- key owners
- decision gates

## Slide 10: Decision required

- decision options
- recommended option
- next step

## Style guidance

- use executive language rather than technical diary notes
- put the recommendation first
- prefer evidence over enthusiasm
- avoid claiming ROI where no baseline exists
- mark assumptions clearly
