# Proposal / scoping document template

Use this template for a formal scoping paper or an equivalent in-chat proposal. It should support a real proceed, reshape, defer, or stop decision rather than acting as a generic concept note.

## Title

`[Initiative name] - AI initiative proposal`

## 1. Executive summary

- one-paragraph summary
- recommendation: proceed to POC, reshape, defer, or do not proceed
- decision required

## 2. Business challenge and opportunity

- the problem being solved
- the current operating model or process
- the business impact
- why the initiative matters now

## 3. Proposed solution

- the AI-assisted capability
- the intended users
- the expected output or operational effect
- what is explicitly out of scope

- success metrics
- strategic fit
- reuse potential
